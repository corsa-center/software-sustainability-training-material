
# 3. Community

3.1. Community engagement & Community management

  * Topics:  
    * The role and importance of community in both doing work and as a signal for possible uptake of the project  
    * Community management doesn't just happen but should be intentional  
    * Community engagement as a series of levels and actions that move a person from one level to the next  
  * Material:  
    * CSCCE [resources](https://www.cscce.org/resources/cpm/) & [report](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3997802)
    * [Mozilla's Best Practices Working Open](https://github.com/mozilla/open-leadership-training-series)

    * Examples / case studies from project/foundation pairings

    * Talks from foundations

3.6. Community health assessment & how to use it
  * Topics  
    * What metrics exist  
    * What do the mean  
    * How can they be collected  
      * Automatable: obtainable from a forge, repository, package ecosystem or automated analysis  
      * Partially automatable: can be collected automatically, but interpretation is required  
      * Human/external evidence: requires maintainers, users or external verification  
    * How can they be used  
    * Maturity models & badges  
  * Material  
    * [CHAOSS](https://www.chaoss.community/))  
    * [https\://www\.foo.be/2026/08/Open-Source-Metrics.html](https://www.foo.be/2026/08/Open-Source-Metrics.html)  
    * CASS [Metrics working group](https://cass.community/working-groups/metrics.html)
    * CASS [Impact framework working group](https://cass.community/working-groups/impact-framework.html)
