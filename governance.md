# 2. Governance

2.1. Governance and roles

  * Topics  
    * Charter/Mission  
    * Organization (leaders, steering committee, etc.)  
    * Decision making  
    * Membership, if appropriate and needed, and if so, joining and leaving criteria and processes  
    * How governance is documented  
    * How governance can be changed  
  * Material  
    * CSCCE [resources](https://www.cscce.org/resources/cpm/) & [report](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3997802)
    * [Mozilla's Best Practices Working Open](https://github.com/mozilla/open-leadership-training-series)
    * [CORSA's collected pointers to governance information](https://github.com/corsa-center/oss-documents#governance)

2.6. Licensing and IP  

  * Topics  
    * Who owns the project and its assets  
      * If all developers are in one institutions, perhaps the institution  
      * A foundation  
      * An organization created by the project  
    * Who can use the assets and for what  
      * CC licenses for images and data  
      * OSI-approved licenses for software  
      * Other licenses  
    * How are disputes handled  
  * Material  
    * [CORSA's collected pointers to governance information](https://github.com/corsa-center/oss-documents#governance)  
    * [OSI licenses](https://opensource.org/licenses)
