# Sources of material

(Note: This material is not very well-organized currently, and would benefit from reorganization. Additional content/links are also welcome.)

* [CSCCE/Birdaro](https\://www\.birdaro.org/) - community-focused, cohort training 
  * [Announcement](https://www.cscce.org/2025/07/01/the-birdaro-training-program-supporting-open-source-leaders/)
  * Note: As of 3 Nov 2026, no public material is available
* [Mozilla/GitHub Open Leaders](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/initiatives/mozilla-open-leaders/) - community-focused, cohort training
  * [Announcement](https://medium.com/read-write-participate/introducing-open-leaders-x-ol%CB%A3-a12e049f5cc0?p=a12e049f5cc0)   
  * [Open Leaders Syllabus](https://mzl.la/olx-syllabus) 
  * [Material](https://github.com/mozilla/open-leadership-training-series)  
* [GitHub's Open Source Guide](https://opensource.guide/)  
* [OLS's Open Science Training](https://we-are-ols.org/open-science-training.html) - materials have an open leadership focus, plans to further develop curriculum to include sustainability, grant readiness, etc.
  * [Seeds to System materials](https://we-are-ols.org/seeds-to-systems-research/) will cover sustainability in projects and next steps
    * Note: As of 3 Nov 2026, no public material is available
* [OSS Sustainability Lab (originally from CHAOSS)](https://oss-sustainability-lab.github.io/website/) - Synchronous 1:1, in-person workshop, asynchronous resources
  * Note: As of 3 Nov 2026, no public material is available
* [Software Carpentry](https://software-carpentry.org)
  * [Managing research software projects class](https://swcarpentry.github.io/managing-research-software-projects/)
* [INTERSECT](https://intersect-training.org) - RSE Bootcamps: week-long, in-person, training events to introduce intermediate/advanced research software developers to Research Software Engineering concepts and techniques
  * [Curriculum and materials](https://intersect-training.org/curriculum/)
  * [Links to other existing material](https://intersect-training.org/training-links/)
* [CodeRefinery](https://coderefinery.org) - teaches all the essential tools that are usually skipped in academic education so everyone can make full use of software, computing, and data with focus on reusability, reproducibility, and openness
  * [Lessons](https://coderefinery.org/lessons/)
* [URSSI (US Research Software Sustainability Institute) Schools](https://github.com/si2-urssi) - aimed at early-career researchers, particularly graduate students and postdocs, who are familiar with basic skills such as interacting with the Unix shell, version control using Git, and Python programming, and would like to learn more about best practices for developing research software
* [UNIVERSE-HPC](https://www.universe-hpc.ac.uk) - a training curriculum framework – spanning levels from undergraduate to continuing professional development - for Research Software Engineers (RSEs) specializing in high performance computing (HPC)
  * [Training material](https://www.universe-hpc.ac.uk/resources/training-material/)
* [IDEAS](https://ideas-productivity.org) - Partnering with the community to advance scientific software practices
  * [Tools & practices](https://ideas-productivity.org/resources/tools-and-practices.html)
  * [BSSw (Better Scientific Software)](https://bssw.io)
* [SGX3/SGCI training](https://sciencegateways.org) - empowers the science gateways community to advance the state of the art in gateway development and operations
  * Offers training in business development, sustainability, but as of 3 Nov 2026, no public material is available
* [I-Corps](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/i-corps) - an immersive, entrepreneurial training program that facilitates the transformation of invention to impact. This seven-week experiential training program prepares scientists and engineers to extend their focus beyond the university laboratory — accelerating the economic and societal benefits of NSF-funded and other basic research projects that are ready to move toward commercialization.
  * Various regional hubs, e.g., [Great Lakes Regional I-Corps Hub](https://greatlakesicorps.org), that offer training and resources
* [S<sup>3</sup> (Sustainable Scientific Software) School](https://indico.in2p3.fr/event/36319/) - providing good coding practices to develop better software for your research
  * [Lectures](https://s3-school.github.io/s3-2026-lectures/)
* ... working
* [The Turing Way](https://book.the-turing-way.org/) - handbook to reproducible, ethical and collaborative data science
* [World Data System Sustainability Summit 2023: Outcomes and Lessons Learned](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8383658)  
* [Princeton's RSEs' handbook](https://handbook.rse.princeton.edu) - a living document that explains the what and why of sustainable research software
  * [Section on software sustainability](https://handbook.rse.princeton.edu/sustainability.html)
    * Note: as of 3 Nov 2026, the sustainability part is mostly characteristics, rather than guidance. Best practices to realize the characteristics are under review internally, and will be released in the future.
